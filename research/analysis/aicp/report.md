## Explicit non-claims

- No style, aesthetic quality, mood, lighting, camera/lens, editorial intent, authorship, identity, or creative-origin inference is made from these numbers.
- Display-RGB luma/saturation and cut candidates are numeric proxies only, not semantic labels.
- Windows are sampled and census values are film-level; a window rate is a sample, not a film rate, and census cut candidates are still scene-score exceedances rather than verified edits.
- Public availability is not a licence: `rightsStatus` remains `review-required`, and reuse needs its own review.
- No award level is claimed: the archive exposes no gold/silver/bronze field, and membership in `commercialsEntries` is the only honoree signal.
- Agency, director, and producer credits are absent for most entries; `company` is reported only where the archive populates it.
- Screenshots are single published images, not video frames, and carry no motion, cut, pacing, or timing evidence.
- Signed CDN redirect targets are never persisted; only the catalog's public HLS entry URL appears in records.
- Catalog metadata (title, year, company, video type, categories) is self-reported by aicpawards.com and is not independently verified.
- Downloaded media, frames, thumbnails, and audio are never retained.
