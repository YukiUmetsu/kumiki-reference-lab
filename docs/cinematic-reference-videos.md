## Priorities for Kumiki's creative vocabulary

1. **Character interaction and emotion:** Alan Becker's Minecraft film, *The Present*, *Sprite Fright* and *Sintel*. Compare readable posing, eye lines, object contact, gesture timing and emotional reveals. Prototype original two-character dialogues with meaningful environmental reactions.
2. **Shot design and cinematic 3D:** *The Call*, *Still Here*, *The OceanMaker* and *Game of Thrones*. Investigate motivated camera movement, spatial continuity, layered lighting and atmospheric depth. Build original camera/lighting recipes suitable for Storybook and export.
3. **Editing and music choreography:** Nike's *You Can't Stop Us*, the three OK Go films, and *Dumb Ways to Die*. Examine whether transitions match physical motion, music beats, action direction or subject silhouette. Build deterministic timing recipes, not copied edits.
4. **Product craft and graphics:** Apple Vision Pro, Volvo *The Epic Split*, *Stranger Things* titles and IBM's *A Boy and His Atom*. Investigate restrained camera, macro materials, focus, typography and committing to one strong visual idea.
5. **Low-budget large-scale worldbuilding:** Ian Hubert's *DYNAMO DREAM*. Compare camera tracking, composited layers, reused modular environments and audio. Determine which principles translate to an original, automated 2.5D/3D asset factory.

## Source, legal and validation boundaries

- Versioned records: [`research/popular-cinematic-videos.v1.json`](../research/popular-cinematic-videos.v1.json), separately from [`research/popular-explainer-videos.v1.json`](../research/popular-explainer-videos.v1.json). Both use the [v1 contract](../schemas/v1/popular-reference-videos.schema.json). Source URLs and snapshot fields are per-video; missing snapshot date is `null` rather than a guessed date.
- **Inspiration only:** linking to a third-party film does not confer rights to distribute its frames, audio, proprietary characters, brand design, typography or assets. Do not download copyrighted source videos as part of this metadata discovery process.
- **Not yet visually validated:** technique tags and study questions are hypotheses. Proper film study requires authorized access, exact frame timestamps and full-speed visual QA before marking a motion recipe as validated.
- Search both cohorts offline with `PYTHONPATH=src python -m kumiki_reference_lab video-references search "character acting"` or `... search "title sequence"`. Use `--root research/popular-cinematic-videos.v1.json` for this cohort only, or `--root research/popular-explainer-videos.v1.json` for the previous 19 films.
- Do not equate popularity with artistic merit. A future stronger study should normalize by age/channel reach and assess framing, pacing and emotional clarity independently of view counts.
